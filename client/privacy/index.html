隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日
必要的只有你的 Discord 名字、頭像和使用者 ID，用來在牌桌上顯示你、斷線時認出你。其他權限都是選擇性的。
遊戲資料只放在伺服器記憶體，房間關掉就刪掉。沒有資料庫。
沒有廣告、沒有追蹤、沒有 Cookie，也不賣或分享你的資料。

1這份政策說的是什麼

MadCards（以下稱「本遊戲」）是在 Discord 語音頻道裡用「活動」玩的多人卡牌遊戲，也可以直接用瀏覽器開。這份政策說明本遊戲會拿到哪些資料、怎麼使用、放多久，以及你可以怎麼做。

2我們拿到哪些資料

  • Discord 帳號資料：在 Discord 裡開啟本遊戲時，會請你授權 identify 權限，我們因此取得你的 Discord 使用者 ID、使用者名稱、顯示名稱和頭像。這是唯一必要的權限。我們拿不到你的 email、密碼、好友名單或訊息。
  • 選擇性的 Discord 權限：授權視窗也會列出下面三項，用來開啟額外功能。你不同意的話照樣能玩，只是對應的功能會關掉：
    • guilds：讓 Discord 允許本遊戲讀取你所在語音頻道的成員名稱和頭像，在大廳列出「在語音頻道裡還沒加入」的人。這個權限在技術上也能讀取你加入的伺服器列表，但本遊戲不會讀取或保存它。
    • rpc.activities.write：在你的 Discord 個人檔案上顯示遊戲狀態（Rich Presence），例如「打牌中、手上還有 5 張」。
    • rpc.voice.read：知道語音頻道裡誰正在說話，在牌桌上亮起麥克風。本遊戲只知道「有沒有在說話」，聽不到、也不會錄下任何聲音。
  • 遊戲資料：你所在的房間、座位、手牌、出的牌、是否喊了 LAST!、連線狀態等，遊戲進行時需要的資料。
  • 瀏覽器直接開的時候：你輸入的名字。
  • 技術資料：你的連線會經過網路服務商（見第 5 點），它們依自己的政策可能會看到你的 IP 位址等連線資訊。開發與測試期間，伺服器的記錄檔可能會出現瀏覽器類型、顯示卡型號、畫面每秒格數等診斷資訊，只用來修正畫面和效能問題。

3我們怎麼使用

  • 在牌桌上顯示你的名字和頭像。
  • 你斷線或重新開啟時，用 Discord 使用者 ID 認出你，把原本的座位和手牌還給你；你離線期間由電腦代打。
  • 你加入房間時，伺服器用你的存取權杖向 Discord 確認你的使用者 ID，避免別人冒充你拿走座位。
  • 你有同意選擇性權限的話：列出語音頻道裡的成員、顯示誰在說話、更新你的 Rich Presence（見第 2 點）。
  • 讓遊戲運作：發牌、判斷規則、把畫面同步給同桌的人。
  • 找出和修正錯誤。

我們不會用你的資料做廣告、行銷或分析側寫，也不會賣給或分享給任何人。

4資料放多久

  • 遊戲資料只存在伺服器的記憶體，不寫進資料庫或檔案。房間裡所有人都離開約 5 分鐘後，房間和裡面的資料就會刪除；在大廳斷線超過 60 秒的座位也會移除。
  • Discord 登入用的存取權杖（access token）只經過伺服器轉交給你的裝置；你加入房間時，伺服器會再用它向 Discord 確認一次你是誰，用完就丟，不會保存。
  • 語音頻道成員的名稱和頭像只在你的裝置上讀取和顯示，不會傳到伺服器。
  • 伺服器記錄檔只用來除錯，不會長期保存。

5第三方服務

  • Discord：提供登入、活動平台和網路代理，適用 Discord 隱私權政策。你的頭像圖片直接從 Discord 的伺服器載入。
  • Cloudflare：本遊戲的網路連線經過 Cloudflare，適用 Cloudflare 隱私權政策。

6同桌的人看得到什麼

同一桌的玩家看得到你的顯示名稱、頭像、手牌張數、你打出的牌、有沒有喊 LAST!，以及你是否在線。其他人看不到你手牌的內容。在 Discord 裡玩的時候，同桌玩家的裝置也會收到你的 Discord 使用者 ID，用來把語音頻道裡的說話狀態對到你的座位上。

在大廳裡，同一個語音頻道的人會看到彼此的名稱和頭像（包括還沒開啟本遊戲的人），這些資訊本來就顯示在 Discord 的語音頻道裡。

7存在你裝置上的東西

本遊戲不使用 Cookie。會在你的瀏覽器存少量設定：是否靜音、畫質、頁面語言（localStorage）；用瀏覽器直接開的時候，還有你的名字和一組分頁代號（sessionStorage，關掉分頁就清除）。這些只存在你的裝置上，你可以隨時在瀏覽器設定裡清除。

8你可以怎麼做

  • 隨時到 Discord 的「使用者設定 → 已授權的應用程式」取消本遊戲的授權。
  • 因為我們不長期保存你的資料，離開遊戲後幾分鐘內資料就會刪除。想確認或詢問任何事，請用第 11 點的聯絡方式找我們。

9兒童

本遊戲透過 Discord 提供，使用者必須符合 Discord 的最低年齡規定（多數地區為 13 歲）。我們不會刻意收集未達這個年齡的兒童的資料。

10政策變更

這份政策如果有修改，會更新這一頁和上方的生效日期。修改後繼續使用本遊戲，表示你接受新的內容。

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 29, 2026
The only required data is your Discord name, avatar and user ID — to show you at the table and recognize you when you reconnect. Every other permission is optional.
Game data lives only in server memory and is deleted when the room closes. No database.
No ads, no tracking, no cookies. We never sell or share your data.

1What this policy covers

MadCards (“the Game”) is a multiplayer card game you play as an Activity in a Discord voice channel, or directly in a web browser. This policy explains what data the Game receives, how it is used, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it.

2Data we receive

  • Discord account data: when you open the Game in Discord, we ask for the identify permission, which gives us your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar. This is the only required permission. We do not get your email, password, friends list or messages.
  • Optional Discord permissions: the consent screen also lists the three permissions below, which turn on extra features. If you decline them you can still play; only that feature is turned off:
    • guilds: lets Discord allow the Game to read the names and avatars of the members of your current voice channel, so the lobby can list people who are in the voice channel but haven’t joined yet. Technically this permission could also read the list of servers you are in, but the Game does not read or store it.
    • rpc.activities.write: shows your game status on your Discord profile (Rich Presence), for example “Playing, 5 cards left”.
    • rpc.voice.read: tells the Game who in the voice channel is speaking, so a microphone lights up at the table. The Game only knows whether someone is speaking; it cannot hear or record any audio.
  • Game data: your room, seat, hand, cards played, LAST! calls and connection status — what the game needs while it is running.
  • When playing in a browser: the name you type in.
  • Technical data: your connection passes through network providers (see section 5), which may see connection information such as your IP address under their own policies. During development and testing, server logs may include diagnostics such as browser type, graphics chip model and frame rate, used only to fix display and performance problems.

3How we use it

  • To show your name and avatar at the table.
  • To recognize you by your Discord user ID when you disconnect or reopen the Game, and give you back your seat and cards. While you are offline, the computer plays for you.
  • When you join a room, the server uses your access token to confirm your user ID with Discord, so nobody else can pretend to be you and take your seat.
  • If you granted the optional permissions: to list the members of your voice channel, show who is speaking, and update your Rich Presence (see section 2).
  • To run the game: dealing cards, enforcing the rules and keeping everyone’s screen in sync.
  • To find and fix bugs.

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing or profiling, and we never sell it or share it with anyone.

4How long we keep it

  • Game data is kept only in server memory — never in a database or file. About 5 minutes after everyone has left a room, the room and all its data are deleted. Seats that stay disconnected in the lobby for more than 60 seconds are removed.
  • The Discord access token used to sign you in passes through the server on its way to your device. When you join a room, the server uses it once more to confirm who you are with Discord, then discards it. The server does not store it.
  • The names and avatars of voice channel members are read and shown only on your device. They are never sent to the server.
  • Server logs are used only for debugging and are not kept long term.

5Third-party services

  • Discord provides sign-in, the Activity platform and the network proxy, under the Discord Privacy Policy. Your avatar image is loaded directly from Discord’s servers.
  • Cloudflare carries the Game’s network traffic, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

6What other players can see

Players at your table can see your display name, avatar, how many cards you hold, the cards you play, whether you called LAST!, and whether you are online. They cannot see what cards are in your hand. When playing in Discord, your tablemates’ devices also receive your Discord user ID, which is used to match who is speaking in the voice channel to your seat.

In the lobby, people in the same voice channel see each other’s names and avatars (including people who haven’t opened the Game yet). This is the same information Discord already shows in the voice channel.

7What is stored on your device

The Game does not use cookies. It saves a few settings in your browser: mute, graphics quality and page language (localStorage). When playing in a browser, it also saves your name and a tab ID (sessionStorage, cleared when you close the tab). These stay on your device and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

8Your choices

  • You can revoke the Game’s access at any time in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • Because we don’t keep your data long term, it is deleted within minutes after you leave. For any question or request, contact us (section 11).

9Children

The Game is offered through Discord, so you must meet Discord’s minimum age (13 in most countries). We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Continuing to use the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
